The [Synopsys tool map source](Synopsys%20RTL%20to%20GDS%20Tool%20Map.md) produces an additional [one-page PDF](../Synopsys%20RTL%20to%20GDS%20Tool%20Map.pdf) with only the process stages and tool names.

All supporting material is kept inside this folder; reader PDFs are directly at the repository top level. Edit the Markdown in [Daily Notes](Daily%20Notes/README.md) to improve an explanation, then rebuild its PDF. Preserve source-page identity, image provenance and the six-lesson day boundaries. Course lesson numbers include tutorials and can differ from the lecturer's printed lecture number.

The [Week 7 and Week 8 practice-question source](Week%2007%20and%2008%20-%20Practice%20Questions.md) produces a separate worksheet: 20 original questions, 161 unmarked options and six original figures. Completing a quiz does not advance the lecture marker.

The [PDF tools guide](tools/pdf/README.md) gives the build order, dependencies and checks. Temporary downloads stay in `.work`; rendered pages, transcripts, numerical reports and review records stay in ignored `tools/pdf/qa`. Maintained content and reader PDFs remain versioned. Historical imports are separated from the routine rebuild workflow.
