The guide covers IC structure and fabrication, design styles, economics, PPA, abstraction, hardware-software partitioning, SoC/IP reuse and Unix. Behavioural synthesis receives a detailed treatment of scheduling, allocation, binding, control, interfaces, resource sharing, advantages and limitations. Worked examples distinguish latency, initiation interval, throughput and clock-period budgets.

Week 1 is four lectures plus Tutorial 1. The existing Day 01 reading PDF also includes the first Week 2 lecture; this revision uses the course-week boundary. Original handwriting and full lecture figures remain in the [Day 01 reading edition](../PDFs/Day%2001%20-%20IC%20Foundations%20and%20Logic%20Synthesis.pdf).
